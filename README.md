# Customer Churn Analysis & Prediction

## 📌 Objective
Analyze customer churn patterns and develop a machine learning model to identify customers who are likely to leave the company. The project combines Python-based data analysis and churn prediction with interactive Power BI dashboards to support data-driven customer retention strategies.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Python:** Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn
- **Data Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn
- **Business Intelligence:** Power BI
- **Data Processing:** Data Cleaning, EDA, Feature Engineering
- **Machine Learning:** Classification and Churn Prediction

## 📊 Key Insights
- Analyzed **6,418 customers**, identifying **1,732 churned customers** and an overall churn rate of **26.99%**.
- Identified **411 new joiners** in the customer base.
- Month-to-month contracts showed the highest churn compared with one-year and two-year contracts.
- Fiber optic customers contributed substantially to churn, highlighting the need to investigate service quality and pricing.
- Competitor offers, better devices, and support-related issues emerged as important reported churn reasons.
- Customer churn patterns varied across tenure groups, age groups, payment methods, and states.
- The prediction dashboard identified **387 predicted churners**, helping highlight customers who may require retention efforts.

## 📈 Dashboard Features
- Customer churn KPIs and churn rate monitoring.
- Churn analysis by gender, age, tenure, state, contract, payment method, and internet type.
- Analysis of customer-reported churn reasons and service subscriptions.
- Customer-level churn prediction report with financial and demographic details.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Encourage longer-term contracts through suitable offers and loyalty benefits.
- Investigate pricing, device offerings, and service-quality concerns compared with competitors.
- Improve customer support and address recurring service complaints.
- Develop targeted retention campaigns for customers identified as high risk.
- Monitor churn rates regularly to evaluate the effectiveness of retention strategies.

## 🎯 Conclusion
This project combines exploratory data analysis, machine learning, and Power BI reporting to understand customer churn and identify potential churners. The insights can help businesses improve customer retention, refine service offerings, and make informed decisions using customer data.
